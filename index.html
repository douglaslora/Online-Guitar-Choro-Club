✦ Session No. 6 — November 14, 2026

Revendo o Passado
Francisco José Freire Júnior (1926)
Brazilian Waltz · 44-page PDF study package included

Exploring the interpretative universe of the Brazilian waltz. The valsa brasileira has a character all its own: singing bass lines, polyphonic counter-lines, flexible rubato and deep lyricism. We dig into the choices that shape an interpretation — accompaniment patterns, harmony, phrasing and tempo. Runs a full 2 hours — 90 minutes on the tune plus 30 minutes live with a special guest.

✦ Special guest — live Q&A

Swami Jr.

One of Brazil’s most prominent seven-string guitarists, and also an arranger and producer. He has recorded and performed with Omara Portuondo, Chico César, Elba Ramalho, Lokua Kanza, Zélia Duncan, Vanessa da Mata, Elza Soares, Zeca Baleiro, Tom Zé, Luciana Souza and Dominguinhos, among many others. He was Omara Portuondo’s musical director on her worldwide tour and co-produced her album Gracias, which won the 2009 Latin Grammy for Best Contemporary Tropical Album. He was also nominated for the 2006 Latin Grammy for Sound Engineering, for Chico César’s De Uns Tempos Pra Cá.

As a composer, his song “Bom Dia” was recorded by Zizi Possi on the award-winning album Valsa Brasileira, and in 2009 he released his solo album Outra Praia on World Village / Harmonia Mundi.
